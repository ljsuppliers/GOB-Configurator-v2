2. Scope of work

The Installer is contracted to fully install the building at the site address, as per the job specification (this job pack: drawing, build summary and materials checklist) and company standards.

The Installer is responsible for arranging and paying for the plastering and decoration of the interior by a plasterer; this is included in the agreed fee. Electrical work is carried out by the Company's electrician and is outside this agreement.

The Company is responsible for supplying all materials and components. Materials arrive in separate deliveries from different suppliers throughout the project, up to around ten deliveries per job, and most are kerbside deliveries. The Installer is responsible for taking each delivery, moving the materials safely into the garden and storing them properly on site, and for checking the materials on delivery and reporting anything damaged or missing to the Company as soon as possible.

3. Agreed price & schedule

Agreed install fee{{ fmt(installerDeal.fee) }} ({{ installerDeal.days }} working days)
Install start{{ fmtDateLong(state.installer && state.installer.startDate) }}

The fee is fixed for the scope above. Any variation requested by the customer or Company on site must be agreed in writing (email is sufficient) before the work is carried out, with a separate price agreed for the additional scope.

4. Payment terms

The agreed fee is payable as follows:

  • 50% halfway through the install, once the building is watertight.
  • 50% on completion.

5. Defects period

The Installer warrants their workmanship for 1 year from completion. Any defects attributable to the installation (as distinct from materials defects, which the Company handles directly with suppliers) will be remedied by the Installer at no charge within 2 weeks of notice.

6. Insurance

The Installer must hold their own valid insurance for the duration of the install. Evidence of cover must be provided to the Company on request.

7. Health & safety

The Installer is responsible for safe working practices on site, including (but not limited to) appropriate PPE, safe handling of panels and timber, electrical safety during fit-out, and securing the site at the end of each working day.
